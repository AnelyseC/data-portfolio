# Business Questions

## Project Context

This project uses the Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset by Olist to analyze customer behavior, order experience, satisfaction, and retention opportunities in a marketplace environment.

The objective is to build a customer analytics project that transforms raw transactional data into business insights that could support decision-making in areas such as customer segmentation, retention, delivery experience, and product/category performance.

## Main Business Question

How can we segment Olist customers and identify the main drivers of customer value, repeat purchase behavior, and satisfaction?

## Supporting Questions

### 1. Customer Profile

- Where are Olist customers located?
- Which states and cities concentrate the largest number of customers?
- How many customers purchased only once versus multiple times?

### 2. Customer Value

- Which customers generate the highest monetary value?
- What is the distribution of customer spending?
- What is the average order value by customer segment?
- Which customer segments contribute most to total revenue?

### 3. Retention and Frequency

- What percentage of customers made more than one purchase?
- How long is the average time between purchases?
- Which customers are recent, frequent, and high-value?
- Which customers appear to be inactive or at risk?

### 4. Delivery Experience

- What percentage of orders were delivered late?
- What is the average delivery time?
- Which states have the highest delivery delays?
- How does delivery delay affect customer satisfaction?

### 5. Customer Satisfaction

- What is the distribution of review scores?
- Are delayed orders associated with lower review scores?
- Which product categories have the highest and lowest satisfaction?
- Which operational factors seem most related to poor reviews?

### 6. Product and Category Behavior

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which categories are most frequently purchased?
- Which categories have higher average order value?
- Which categories are associated with better or worse reviews?

## Expected Outputs

The project should produce:

- A cleaned Silver data layer.
- A Gold analytical data model.
- Customer-level features.
- RFM-based customer segmentation.
- Delivery and satisfaction analysis.
- A Power BI dashboard.
- A final GitHub README explaining the business problem, methodology, insights, and recommendations.

## Business Value

This analysis can help an e-commerce marketplace:

- Understand its customer base.
- Identify high-value customer groups.
- Detect retention opportunities.
- Prioritize operational improvements in delivery.
- Understand how customer experience affects satisfaction.
- Support more targeted CRM and customer success actions.
